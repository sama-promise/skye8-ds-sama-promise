By neighbourhood/property type: Median rents vary substantially by neighbourhood (e.g. Bastos ≈280k XAF vs. PK14 ≈109k XAF) but are fairly flat across property types (~160–171k XAF median regardless of apartment/studio/villa/etc.) — location, not property type, is the bigger driver of price here.

Douala vs. Yaounde: Douala's median rent is 18,433 XAF higher than Yaounde's (Bonferroni-adjusted 98.3% CI: [8,218, 27,998]) — a real difference.

Generators: Flats with a generator show 36,649 XAF higher median rent (adjusted CI: [25,524, 48,541]). But generators are extremely non-randomly distributed across neighbourhoods (χ² p ≈ 3.8×10⁻¹²⁷) — so this raw comparison is confounded with location, and the true generator effect (if any) can only be isolated once neighbourhood is controlled for in the regression.

Multiple comparisons: 3 statistical tests were run in this stage (city median difference, generator median difference, generator–neighbourhood association); a Bonferroni correction (α = 0.05/3) was applied throughout, and all findings remain significant at the corrected threshold.
