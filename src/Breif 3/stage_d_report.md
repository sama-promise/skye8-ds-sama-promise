bedrooms and area_sqm are strongly collinear (r = 0.886), with elevated VIFs (5.67 and 4.78 respectively — both approaching the commonly-cited concern threshold of 5). Fitting them together versus alone shows the instability directly: bedrooms's coefficient nearly triples (0.148 → 0.413) once area_sqm is removed, because the two variables share overlapping information about unit size and compete for credit when both are present. Standard errors are correspondingly smaller in the single-predictor models. Rather than dropping either outright — both carry genuinely different information (room count vs. precise floor area) — both are retained in the final model with this instability explicitly disclosed, since removing area_sqm would lose the more granular size signal and removing bedrooms would lose the room-count effect landlords/tenants specifically care about.

243 listings (4.3%) were flagged as influential by Cook's distance (threshold 4/n). Refitting without them shows the core size predictors (area_sqm, bedrooms) move by 10–16%, while the largest percentage swings occur in coefficients that were already statistically insignificant in the full model (floor, property_type categories) and represent negligible absolute changes. The influential points are largely the same large/expensive listings identified as extreme in Stage A — real, valid observations of the top end of the market, not data errors. Removing them would make the model less representative of exactly the segment (large, expensive properties) where the agency most needs a defensible estimate. Decision: retain all listings in the final model, with this sensitivity check disclosed so the agency understands the estimate is somewhat conditioned on including these high-end properties.

Holdout validation on a 20% test split (model refit on the remaining 80%) gives MAE of 49,585 XAF, RMSE of 121,597 XAF, and mean absolute percentage error of 21.9%. The gap between MAE and RMSE indicates a subset of large errors — consistent with the influential, high-value listings identified above — pulling RMSE well above the typical (MAE) error. This is the error the agency will actually feel when quoting a landlord: on a mid-range flat renting for ~170,000 XAF, a typical miss is in the range of 35,000–50,000 XAF.
