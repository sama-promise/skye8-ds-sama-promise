The raw model's R² measures the proportion of variance explained in monthly_rent_xaf (francs). The log model's R² measures variance explained in log(monthly_rent_xaf) — a different target on a different scale. A higher R² here doesn't mean "the log model explains 16 more percentage points of rent" — it means the log model fits its (transformed) target better, which is expected once the skew is addressed, but the two numbers aren't measuring the same quantity and shouldn't be read as directly competing scores.
