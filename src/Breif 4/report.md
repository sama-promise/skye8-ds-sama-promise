A quick frozen-feature approach (no fine-tuning) failed to beat the baseline and was unstable — showing that simply reusing an off-the-shelf image model's features isn't enough for this task. The measurement-based model (22.5 kg MAE) remains the strongest validated result so far, within the published benchmark range. The next real step is genuine fine-tuning — actually training a CNN's weights end-to-end on cattle photos and weights — which is a meaningfully different (and more involved) approach than what was tested here.
